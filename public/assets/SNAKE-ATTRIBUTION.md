# Active contribution snake

Animated Snake by Calciumtrice.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-snake
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)

Original, unmodified file: snake spritesheet calciumtrice.png (320 x 160).
Five rows of ten 32 x 32 frames: idle, gesture, walk, attack, death.
The portfolio displays idle, walk, and the full attack cycle for eating
contributions, scaled 2x and mirrored to face the direction of travel.
Visible attribution is included below the animation controls.
